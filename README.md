# THE BULLION POST — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A one-page financial front page for Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn entirely
in code: gold bullion bars and dark chocolate browns, a classic grid, and the
markets up front without letting the hard news fall off the page.

The lead trio is the market story of the day — Nvidia bringing back Jensen
Huang as CEO (+31%), gold rebounding 1.5% to $3,588 an ounce, and Bitcoin
steadying near $61,284 — while the grid above the fold still carries the
Merkury sinking off Benghazi, the Kyiv strikes, the COP31 no-deal collapse
and Gaza in full. In total: 34 dated stories across 9 regions, an 8-figure
numbers strip tied to its cards, a UTC timeline with honestly labelled
Monday-evening slots, a dotted world map whose numbered pins match the story
numbers, and a DATE CHECK box naming the older stories deliberately left out.

`main.py` is a standard-library-only carrier: it copies the bundled
`entry.png` (1024×1024) to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, every run.
The picture was composed with a local PIL script (`compose_bullion.py`);
all figures come from the day's wire pool (Reuters, AP, BBC, CNN, Times,
Treasury, CoinPaprika, Gadgets360 and others, as credited on the page).
